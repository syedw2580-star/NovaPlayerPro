3. WEB AUDIO DSP ENGINE (AudioEngine)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AudioEngine class manages the Web Audio API lifecycle to overcome browser limitations
on surround sound and volume dynamics:

A. 6-Channel 5.1 Surround Sound Matrix Downmixer:
   Standard HTML5 video elements often mute or drop Channel 2 (Center/Dialogue) on 5.1 AAC
   tracks when outputting to 2-channel stereo headphones/speakers. 
   Nova Player Pro uses an explicit 6-channel ChannelSplitterNode(6):
     - Channel 0: Left Front
     - Channel 1: Right Front
     - Channel 2: Center (Dialogue) — matrix-mixed into L & R at 0.707 gain (-3dB).
     - Channel 3: Subwoofer (LFE) — mixed into L & R at 0.5 gain.
     - Channel 4: Left Surround — mixed into L at 0.707 gain.
     - Channel 5: Right Surround — mixed into R at 0.707 gain.
   Re-combined into a 2-channel ChannelMergerNode(2) with explicit channelCount = 2 and
   channelCountMode = 'explicit'.

B. Smart Audio Booster & Soft-Clipping Limiter (300% Safe Loudness):
   A 2-stage dynamic compression and soft-clipping limiter node chain:
     1. DynamicsCompressorNode:
        - Threshold: -24 dB
        - Knee: 30 dB
        - Ratio: 12:1
        - Attack: 0.003s | Release: 0.25s
        Pulls down loud spikes while boosting faint dialogue by up to +18 dB.
     2. WaveShaperNode Limiter:
        Uses a hyperbolic tangent (Math.tanh(x * 1.5)) transfer curve across 44,100 precomputed
        samples. Hard-clamps maximum output amplitude at 0 dBFS (+1.0 amplitude) to eliminate
        digital clipping or speaker crackle even at 300% volume.

C. 3-Band Equalizer:
   Low-shelf (Bass @ 60 Hz), Peaking (Mid @ 1000 Hz), High-shelf (Treble @ 10000 Hz).
   Presets: Flat, Bass Boost (+7dB), Vocal (+6dB Mid), Cinema (+5dB Bass / +4dB Highs), Treble (+8dB).

D. CORS Auto-Detection & Fallback Security Sandbox:
   Detects local file:// or blob: URLs that trigger browser Web Audio CORS security blocks
   and provides seamless fallback to standard HTML5 audio elements.
